About Our Approach

Measurement Before Assumption.

Marine fuel represents a significant operational and financial exposure for vessel owners and operators.

Our approach focuses on physical measurement, document verification, calculation, reconciliation and clear reporting.

Our Services

Marine Fuel Survey Services

Structured services for bunker quantity verification, onboard fuel measurement and fuel data reconciliation.

Bunker Quantity Survey

Independent verification of bunker quantity delivered to the receiving vessel using documented measurement and calculation procedures.

02 / ROB

ROB Verification

Sounding & Ullage

Physical measurement of fuel tank levels supported by appropriate tank calibration and measurement records.

06 / RECONCILIATION

Fuel Reconciliation

Comparison of opening ROB, bunker received, fuel consumption and closing ROB to identify variances.

Fuel Quantity Control

Opening ROB + Bunker Received − Consumption ± Adjustment = Expected Closing ROB
Variance = Actual Closing ROB − Expected Closing ROB

The purpose of reconciliation is not simply to produce a number, but to understand the basis of that number and identify unexplained differences.
